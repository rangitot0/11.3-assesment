Quiz

Answer in your head first, then open the answer to check. No cheating.

1. What was the atmosphere of prebiotic Earth like?

a) Mostly oxygen, like today
b) A reducing atmosphere with no free oxygen
c) There was no atmosphere

Open the answer

b. Reducing means no free oxygen. Lesson 1.

2. Name two energy sources on prebiotic Earth.

Open the answer

Any two of volcanoes, lightning, UV from the sun. Lesson 1.

3. What did Miller-Urey actually produce?

a) Living cells
b) Amino acids
c) DNA

Open the answer

b. Amino acids. Not cells, not DNA. Lesson 2.

4. What did the electrodes represent?

a) The sun
b) Lightning
c) Volcanoes

Open the answer

b. Lightning. Lesson 2.

5. Whats the quickest way to tell prokaryotic and eukaryotic apart?

a) How big the cell is
b) If it has a cell wall
c) If the genetic material is in a membrane-bound nucleus

Open the answer

c. In a nucleus is eukaryotic, loose is prokaryotic. Lesson 3.

6. Name all seven life processes.

Open the answer

MRS GREN. Movement, Respiration, Sensitivity, Growth, Reproduction, Excretion, Nutrition. Lesson 3.

7. Why is binary fission asexual?

a) One parent and the offspring are identical
b) Because its fast
c) Because it happens in the dark

Open the answer

a. One parent, two identical cells, no variation. Lesson 4.

8. Why is it called EXTRAcellular digestion?

Open the answer

Because the enzymes digest the food outside the cell. Lesson 4.

9. Write the word equation for anaerobic respiration in fungi.

Open the answer

glucose → ethanol + carbon dioxide (+ energy). Lesson 4.
