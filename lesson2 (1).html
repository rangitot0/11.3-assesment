Lesson 2 - Miller-Urey

Why they did it

Oparin-Haldane was a hypothesis. Nobody had showed that the building blocks could actually form on their own so in 1952 Miller and Urey built an experiment to test it.

What it did

The 4 simulations:

Flask of water, heated to make vapour: ocean.

Gases added were methane, ammonia and hydrogen: early atmosphere with no oxygen

Electrodes fired sparks through the gas: these were the lightning.

A condenser cooled it back to liquid so they could test it.

The result

After a week the liquid went brown and when they tested it there was amino acids in it wich are the building blocks of protein.

Therefore it proved step 2 of Oparin-Haldane can really happen in appropriate conditions.
