Lesson 3 - Cells and MRS GREN

What a cell is

A cell is the basic unit of all living things. Basic means you cant go any smaller and still have something alive.

Cell theory

The 3 parts

1. All living things are made of one or more cells.

2. The cell is the basic unit of structure and function.

3. All cells come from other cells that already exist.

Part 3 is the intresting one. If cells only come from cells, then where did the first one come from?

MRS GREN

The 7 life processes

Movement

Respiration

Sensitivity

Growth

Reproduction

Excretion

Nutrition

An organims that is "alive" has to do all seven to count as living. Thats why a virus isnt alive: because it cant reproduce by itself.

Prokaryotic vs eukaryotic

Prokaryotic cells have no membrane-bound nucleus and no membrane-bound organelles. The genetic material floats loose in the cytoplasm. Bacteria are the most common type of prokarytoic.

Eukaryotic cells have a membrane-bound nucleus with the genetic material in it. Fungi, protists, plants and animals are all eukaryotic.
