Define Oparin-Haldene

A hypothesis suggesting that life arose gradually from non-living inorganic molecules, with simple building blocks (like amino acids) forming first, which then combined to make complex polymers.

Define Eukaryotic cells.

Cells that possess a membrane-bound nucleus (containing the genetic material) and other membrane-bound organelles (like mitochondria). Fungi, protists, plants, and animals have these cells.

Define Miller-Urey experiment

An experiment carried out in 1952 that simulated the conditions of the early, prebiotic Earth and successfully produced amino acids (the building blocks of protein) from inorganic materials.

Define Prokaryotic cells

Cells that lack a membrane-bound nucleus and other membrane-bound organelles. Bacteria are the primary example.

Define a Cell

The basic structural, functional, and biological unit of all known living organisms.

Define Prebiotic Earth

The conditions of the early Earth before life arose, generally characterized by high energy (volcanoes, lightning) and a reducing atmosphere (lacking free oxygen).

Define cell theory

All living things are made of cells, the cell is the basic unit of life, and cells only come from other cells.

What does MRS GREN stand for

Movement, Respiration, Sensitivity, Growth, Reproduction, Excretion, Nutrition. All seven means its alive.

Define microorganism

A living thing too small to see without a microscope, like bacteria and fungi.

Define binary fission

How bacteria reproduce. The DNA is copied and the cell splits into two identical ones. Its asexual so theres no variation.

Define extracellular digestion

How bacteria and fungi feed. Enzymes go out onto the food, break it down outside the cell, then the small bits get absorbed.

Define fermentation

Anaerobic respiration in yeast. Glucose breaks down without oxygen and makes ethanol and carbon dioxide.
